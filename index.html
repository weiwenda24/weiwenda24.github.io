About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Nanjing University and a member of the LAMDA Group, within the School of Artificial Intelligence. I am advised by Professor Yu-Feng Li, and am also fortunate to be co-advised by Prof. James T. Kwok (HKUST). My research centers on the theory and algorithms of Memory-efficient training for LLMs, and I also work on abductive learning and other neuro-symbolic frameworks.
